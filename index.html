5 Golden Rules for Breakdown Safety
  • 1
    Turn ON Hazard Warning Lights immediately to alert traffic.
  • 2
    Guide vehicle smoothly to the furthest left shoulder or safe curb.
  • 3
    Place Reflective Warning Triangle 45-50 meters behind your vehicle.
  • 4
    Never stand behind the car or on the road; stay behind highway guardrails.
  • 5
    Exit vehicle safely using the passenger-side door away from traffic flow.
